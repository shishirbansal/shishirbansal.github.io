Advanced

Minimum spanning tree, union-find, bipartite check, graph coloring basics.

Learning Flow

Decision sequence

1. Decide graph representation

Adjacency list is the default unless dense matrix behavior matters.

2. Check whether weights exist

Unweighted often means BFS. Non-negative weights often mean Dijkstra.

3. Check whether you need reachability or global structure

Traversal answers reachability. MST or SCC answers structural questions.
